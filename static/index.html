A fairness-first taste decision agent

Find the choice neither person has to fake liking.

Two people can have different taste without needing an average-person recommendation. CommonGround grounds each profile separately, checks the overlap, widens the search when the bridge is weak, and then ranks choices by how well the less enthusiastic person is still served.

1 interpret tastes 2 ground independently 3 stress-test overlap 4 adapt if weak 5 choose bridge
Decision rule
Protect the weaker affinity.

A 95% / 35% option should not beat an 80% / 78% option just because its average looks acceptable. CommonGround makes that trade-off visible.
